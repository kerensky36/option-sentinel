IV Rank × 50%

Yield Score × 30%
Annualised premium yield as % of stock price, normalised to 0–100. A 20%+ annualised yield scores 100. Shown in green when ≥10%.

Targets strikes near 0.25 delta — balancing premium income against assignment risk. Higher delta means deeper ITM and higher assignment risk; scores near 0.
