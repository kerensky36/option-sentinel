Option Sentinel

How we use your data

This is the complete list of every way Option Sentinel uses your data. If a feature changes how data is used, this page is updated in the same release.

Use Data Sent to Why Kept for
Schwab login You sign in on Schwab's own site; Option Sentinel never sees your Schwab username or password. A one-time code comes back to our server. Schwab (api.schwabapi.com) To exchange the one-time code for an access token. The code is used once and discarded. Nothing is stored on the server.
Schwab access token The token that lets the app read your positions. Your browser (sessionStorage). Sent to our server in the Authorization header on each request, which forwards it to Schwab. To fetch your accounts, positions, and option chains from Schwab. Until you close the tab, log out, or click Erase All Data. Never stored or logged on the server.
Account selection The encrypted account hash of the Schwab account you picked. Your browser (sessionStorage); sent to our server and Schwab on each request. To show the right account's positions. Until you close the tab. Never logged on the server.
Positions Your open option positions, Greeks, P&L, and underlying prices, as returned by Schwab, plus about two months of daily closing prices for each underlying you hold (public market data), used to calculate its realised volatility. Fetched from Schwab through our server; cached in your browser (sessionStorage) together with the calculated figures and the time of the refresh. To display the dashboard and payoff graphs, and to calculate each position's fundamentals (realised volatility, moneyness, expected move, probability of finishing in the money, dollar Greeks). Browser cache: until you close the tab. Server: only for the duration of the request.
Covered call screener Your long stock positions, option chains for them, and daily closing prices used to compare implied with realised volatility. Fetched from Schwab through our server; cached in your browser (sessionStorage). To rank covered-call opportunities. Browser cache: until you close the tab. Server: only for the duration of the request.
Server processing All of the above while a request is being handled. Our server on Google Cloud Run. To talk to Schwab and compute Greeks and screener scores. In memory for one request only. No database, no disk, no cache.
Security logs Event type (e.g. invalid token), request path, time, and a one-way hash of your IP address. Google Cloud Logging for our Cloud Run service. To detect abuse and attacks. Per the operator's Cloud Logging retention. Never contains tokens, account data, or positions.
Quorum — Google Vertex AI Only when you request a quorum vote on a position: that position's underlying symbol, option type, strike, expiry, days to expiry, quantity, cost, mark, unrealised P&L, Greeks, implied volatility, and underlying price; the figures calculated from them (realised volatility, IV relative to realised volatility, moneyness, expected move, probability of finishing in the money, dollar Greeks, breakevens, maximum profit and loss, share of maximum profit captured, daily time decay); the time when your positions were last refreshed; and, for the news analyst only, public news headlines. Your browser sends these from its last positions refresh. Your account hash is not sent with a quorum request. No user-identifiable or pedigree data is ever sent — no name, email, address, phone, date of birth, tax ID, account number, account hash, token, or IP address. Google Gemini on Vertex AI, in the operator's own Google Cloud project, including Google Search grounding for the research step (which is given only the ticker symbol, today's date, and the position's expiry date). So five AI analysts can vote CLOSE / HOLD / ROLL. Informational only — not financial advice; no trade is ever placed. Not stored by Option Sentinel on the server or in your browser. Closing the result panel discards it. Google processes it under the operator's Google Cloud terms.
Quorum summary — Google Vertex AI Only after a quorum has voted: the five analysts' votes and rationales, the vote count, and the same position figures listed above, by name. It is one additional request per quorum. No user-identifiable or pedigree data is sent — no account number, account hash, token, or IP address. Google Gemini on Vertex AI, in the operator's own Google Cloud project. To write a one-paragraph summary of the quorum's reasoning. The verdict itself is counted by our server, not by the model. Informational only — not financial advice. Not stored by Option Sentinel on the server or in your browser. Closing the result panel discards it. Google processes it under the operator's Google Cloud terms.
Quorum results — your browser The quorum's verdict, votes, rationales, headlines and summary for each position you asked about. Nowhere. Kept in your browser's sessionStorage and never sent back to our server or anyone else. So asking again about the same position shows the same result instantly instead of running a new quorum. Until you sign out, use Erase All Data, or close the tab. A position whose legs change gets a fresh quorum.
Demo mode Simulated demo accounts and positions built into the page, plus your demo selections (account, screener profile, cached results). Your Schwab token, if you connected one, stays stored while demo mode is on so the Demo | Live switch can bring your live view back without signing in again. Nowhere. Demo data never leaves your browser, and no demo request carries your Schwab token. Demo state is kept in sessionStorage under separate keys and is never shown in live mode. So you can switch between demo and live data and find each exactly as you left it. Until you sign out, use Erase All Data, or close the tab — for both modes.
Quorum — news feeds Only the underlying ticker symbol (for Yahoo Finance's per-ticker feed). The other feeds receive no data about you. CNBC, Yahoo Finance, and Bloomberg public RSS feeds, requested by our server. To gather recent (last 48 hours) market headlines for the quorum's news analyst. Headlines are held in memory for one request and shown in the result panel only.
Page assets Your browser's normal request data (IP address, user agent) when loading the font. fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com. To load the page's font. All styles and scripts are served by Option Sentinel itself. Per those providers' policies. No trading data is ever sent to them.

Option Sentinel has no database and writes nothing about you to disk. Everything in your browser lives in sessionStorage and is erased when you close the tab, or at once with Erase All Data.
