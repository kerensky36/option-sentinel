Option Sentinel

How we use your data

This is the complete list of every way Option Sentinel uses your data. If a feature changes how data is used, this page is updated in the same release.

Use Data Sent to Why Kept for
Schwab login You sign in on Schwab's own site; Option Sentinel never sees your Schwab username or password. A one-time code comes back to our server. Schwab (api.schwabapi.com) To exchange the one-time code for an access token. The code is used once and discarded. Nothing is stored on the server.
Schwab access token The token that lets the app read your positions. Your browser (sessionStorage). Sent to our server in the Authorization header on each request, which forwards it to Schwab. To fetch your accounts, positions, and option chains from Schwab. Until you close the tab, log out, or click Erase All Data. Never stored or logged on the server.
Account selection The encrypted account hash of the Schwab account you picked. Your browser (sessionStorage); sent to our server and Schwab on each request. To show the right account's positions. Until you close the tab. Never logged on the server.
Positions Your open option positions, Greeks, P&L, and underlying prices, as returned by Schwab. Fetched from Schwab through our server; cached in your browser (sessionStorage). To display the dashboard and payoff graphs. Browser cache: until you close the tab. Server: only for the duration of the request.
Covered call screener Your long stock positions and option chains for them. Fetched from Schwab through our server; cached in your browser (sessionStorage). To rank covered-call opportunities. Browser cache: until you close the tab. Server: only for the duration of the request.
Server processing All of the above while a request is being handled. Our server on Google Cloud Run. To talk to Schwab and compute Greeks and screener scores. In memory for one request only. No database, no disk, no cache.
Security logs Event type (e.g. invalid token), request path, time, and a one-way hash of your IP address. Google Cloud Logging for our Cloud Run service. To detect abuse and attacks. Per the operator's Cloud Logging retention. Never contains tokens, account data, or positions.
Quorum — Google Vertex AI Only when you click Quorum on a position: that position's underlying symbol, option type, strike, expiry, days to expiry, quantity, cost, mark, unrealised P&L, Greeks, implied volatility, and underlying price, plus public news headlines. No user-identifiable or pedigree data is ever sent — no name, email, address, phone, date of birth, tax ID, account number, account hash, token, or IP address. Google Gemini on Vertex AI, in the operator's own Google Cloud project, including Google Search grounding for the macro research step (which is given only the ticker symbol and today's date). So five AI analysts can vote CLOSE / HOLD / ROLL. Informational only — not financial advice; no trade is ever placed. Not stored by Option Sentinel on the server or in your browser. Closing the result panel discards it. Google processes it under the operator's Google Cloud terms.
Quorum — news feeds Only the underlying ticker symbol (for Yahoo Finance's per-ticker feed). The other feeds receive no data about you. CNBC, Yahoo Finance, and Bloomberg public RSS feeds, requested by our server. To gather current macro and market headlines for the quorum. Headlines are held in memory for one request and shown in the result panel only.
Page assets Your browser's normal request data (IP address, user agent) when loading styles and fonts. cdn.tailwindcss.com, fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com. To load the page's styling and font. Per those providers' policies. No trading data is ever sent to them.

Option Sentinel has no database and writes nothing about you to disk. Everything in your browser lives in sessionStorage and is erased when you close the tab, or at once with Erase All Data.
